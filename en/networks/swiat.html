SWIAT
European Institutional Asset Chain

Permissioned DLT infrastructure used by European institutions to issue and settle tokenised assets with DvP synchronised with central bank money.

Blockchain for the issuance, circulation and settlement (DvP) of tokenised assets across the European market, validated by central banks.

View Architecture Pricing Institutional

SWIAT's Mission

Bring the benefits of DLT automation to capital markets while natively meeting European regulatory requirements (MiCA, eWpG).

Banking Permissioned Network
Only regulated participants (banks, custodians, FMIs) can operate validator nodes. No anonymity: KYC/AML is built into the network layer by design.
On-Chain DvP Settlement
SWIAT integrates with cash-ledger solutions (such as the Bundesbank's) to enable "T+0 delivery-versus-payment" settlement without principal risk.
Native eWpG Compliance
The architecture technically supports the German legal roles defined by the eWpG law, such as the registrar of electronic securities (Kryptowertpapierregisterführer).

Legal Status & Governance

Who controls the network? A consortium structure for maximum security.

Network Type
Permissioned blockchain. Only consortium members and approved regulated entities can take part in validation.
Validator Access
Consortium model. Nodes are operated by major financial institutions that ensure the integrity of the system.
Eurosystem Interaction
Direct connectivity with the Bundesbank trigger solutions for settlement in central bank money (Cash Layer).

Operating Model & Participants

Unlike public DLTs, SWIAT keeps a strict segregation of roles to replicate traditional market controls on a blockchain.

Legal / Technical Role Responsibility on the SWIAT Network Traditional Equivalent
Issuer Orders the issuance of the tokens representing the bonds. Defines the terms (coupon, maturity). Corporate / Bank Treasury
Tenant Node Operator Operates a SWIAT blockchain node, submits and validates transactions (permissioned consensus). - N/A (New technical role) -
Registrar (eWpG) The crypto-registrar legally responsible for the distributed register of securities. CSD (Central Securities Depository)
Custody Provider Holds private keys (wallets) on behalf of the investor and handles settlement. Custodian Bank (Global Custodian)
