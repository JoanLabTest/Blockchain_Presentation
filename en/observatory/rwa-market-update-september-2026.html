RWA Market Update: $12.88B Tokenized Treasuries

DCM Core Observatory September 2026 Telemetry Report: Institutional tokenized US Treasury AUM reaches $12.88B milestone, driven by BlackRock BUIDL ($2.80B) and Franklin Templeton BENJI ($2.44B).

Observatory Intelligence Brief

As of September 2026, total tokenized real-world assets (RWA) track at $30.0B, led by the Tokenized US Treasury Bills sector at $12.88B. Market consolidation remains concentrated in top institutional vehicles: BlackRock BUIDL ($2.80B) and Franklin Templeton BENJI ($2.44B) together represent over 40% of the total treasury sector volume.

3. Analytical Takeaway

Collateral Utility Driving Growth

DCM Core Observatory Insight: RWA tokenized treasuries are no longer passive yield instruments; they function as active margin collateral on DLT derivatives venues, repo facilities, and wholesale settlement networks. This utility multiplier explains why AUM accelerated past $12.8B in Q3 2026.
