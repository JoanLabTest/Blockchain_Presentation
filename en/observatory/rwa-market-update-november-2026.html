RWA Market Update: November 2026 Telemetry

Consolidated market intelligence on Real-World Asset tokenization: total on-chain assets surpass $39.2B, tokenized Treasuries consolidate at $14.1B, and private credit & emerging debt drive institutional expansion.

Executive Summary

In November 2026, the global tokenized Real-World Asset (RWA) market crossed $39.2 billion in total on-chain capitalization, up from $34.5 billion in September. Tokenized sovereign treasuries reached $14.1 billion, underpinned by BlackRock's BUIDL ($2.95B) and Franklin Templeton's BENJI ($2.55B). According to Stobox's October 2026 industry audit, asset tokenization is transitioning from monolithic money-market parking into diversified multi-asset debt syndication, real estate collateralization, and public corporate equity.

1. Market Aggregates & Benchmark Audit

Telemetry from RWA.xyz and Stobox October Recap

The institutional RWA ecosystem experienced sustained expansion throughout October and early November 2026. The Stobox October 2026 Recap emphasizes an accelerating migration of traditional mid-market corporate debt and structured real estate syndicates onto programmable rails. Concurrently, data compiled from RWA.xyz verifies that total active value locked in on-chain assets reached $39.2 billion, reflecting durable institutional demand that remains largely uncorrelated with broader crypto-asset volatility.

While U.S. Treasury vehicles formed the original foundation of this cycle, secondary market velocity and composability have become the dominant performance metrics. The market has moved beyond passive accumulation: tokenized securities are now actively mobilized across collateral management workflows, margin replenishment on institutional ATS venues, and automated liquidity vaults.

2. Product Leaderboard

Tier-1 Sovereign & Credit Vehicle Concentration

Vehicle concentration within the tokenized treasury vertical remains high, but competitive fragmentation is intensifying as tier-one banks and global asset managers initiate their own native share classes. BlackRock’s BUIDL maintains its lead at $2.95 billion, supported by deep integrations across Circle's collateral ecosystem and institutional custodians. Franklin Templeton’s BENJI on Stellar and Polygon follows closely at $2.55 billion, benefiting from its multi-chain retail accessibility and registered 1940 Act status.

Ondo Finance (USDY and OUSG) maintains aggregate institutional balances exceeding $1.65 billion, capturing yield from short-duration repo and Treasury bills. Meanwhile, the launch of DTCC’s Digital Tokenization Service (DTS) and Schroders' entry into emerging market bonds on Solana illustrate that the competitive surface is no longer confined to risk-free cash vehicles.

3. Multi-Chain Footprint

Infrastructure Partitioning Across Settlement Layers

The distribution of RWA value across underlying blockchains highlights a pragmatic institutional division of labor. Ethereum Mainnet remains the settlement anchor for high-value sovereign paper and institutional collateral, representing over 62% of outstanding tokenized treasuries. However, specialized sub-networks and high-performance execution layers are capturing substantial marginal volume:

Canton Network: Now serves as the private clearing fabric for the DTCC DTS, linking twenty primary financial institutions with zero public transaction leakages.

Solana: Emerging as the venue of choice for active asset managers (exemplified by Schroders ISF) seeking high transaction throughput, minimal latency, and global distribution without EVM gas volatility.

Stellar & Base: Driving fractional liquidity and cross-border payment integration, providing institutional-grade settlement for non-U.S. dollar denominated assets and SME credit pools.
