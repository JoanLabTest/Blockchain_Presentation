Regulatory Transparency Framework

Institutional trust requires absolute clarity on our product's scope, the nature of our metrics and the limits of our risk models.

What DCMCore does

Active Scope
  • On-chain Data Aggregation: We collect, verify and index public and private DLT data.
  • Regulatory Mapping: We map the technical risks of distributed ledgers to the requirements of regulatory texts (MiCA, DORA).
  • Risk Metric Generation (MRM): We provide quantitative indicators designed to support the assessment of digital operational resilience (ICT risk).
  • Immutable Audit Trails: We generate cryptographic proofs (Zero-Trust Anchor) to trace the provenance of decision data.

What we do not do

Out of Scope
  • Legal or Compliance Advice: Our regulatory mappings are technical decision-support tools and are no substitute for legal advice from a specialist firm or formal validation by the regulator.
  • Audit de Code Externe : Our platform is not a smart contract auditor. We measure the state of the network, not the intrinsic security of third-party code.
  • Investment Advice: The "Yield Mechanics" and return calculations (RWA, covered bonds) are structural simulations, not treasury management or investment recommendations.

Simulations and Forward-Looking Models

Methodology

1. Conservative Assumptions

All our quantitative use cases (e.g. Tokenized Bond Capital Buffer) intentionally use conservative assumptions. Coverage ratios, estimated capital requirements and infrastructure costs are smoothed to avoid overstating the benefits of moving on-chain.

2. Forward-Looking Nature of the Data

Part of our documentation (Economic Model, Series A Narrative) includes forward-looking projections on the tokenization market (TAM/SAM). This information is based on public data (EBA and regulator reports) and our own exploratory institutional discussions, and is subject to unavoidable macroeconomic variation.

3. Environnement Sandbox

The publicly accessible demonstration infrastructure runs in "sandbox" mode with partly synthetic data flows to illustrate governance mechanics. Client production environments are strictly on-premise or ring-fenced cloud ("SOC2-ready").
