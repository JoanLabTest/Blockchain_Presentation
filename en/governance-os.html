The Governed
Insight Architecture™

An institutional Model Risk Management (MRM) framework for overseeing digital asset risk: moving from experimental AI tools to a strict, deterministic and auditable intelligence layer.

Separating the Quant & the Narrative

Our architecture is designed to minimise hallucinations: the intelligence layer is structurally barred from making autonomous mathematical estimates or retrieving unverified external data.

Moteur DCM-Risk v2

Deterministic baseline-state calculations vs shock assumptions from raw asset metrics.

Injecteur de Contexte

Passes pure, logged mathematical variance states securely into the prompt boundary.

AI Copilot v1.2

Restricted narrative generation producing committee-ready formats (e.g. 60-second executive committee brief).

Immutable Audit Trails

Every narrative the system generates is time-stamped and cryptographically linked (SHA-256 hash) to the specific scenario data present at generation time. This creates a tamper-evident MRM audit trail designed to support external audit reviews.

[2026-06-21T14:30:00Z] USER: CRO_01
ACTION: AI_COPILOT_GENERATION (COMEX_BRIEF)
HASH: SHA256:8F3A2C...D91E

Deterministic Triggers

Proactive signals are not randomly generated by "AI instinct". The interface logic stays fully deterministic: if a simulated stress variance crosses strictly defined safety buffers (e.g. a drop of 15% or more), the Copilot is called algorithmically to propose a supervisory note.

Built-in Prudent Language

All generated insights are wrapped in explicit, persistent legal disclaimers. The formatting clearly separates analytical summaries from binding investment or liquidity mandates, reducing liability at system level.

Dual-Versioning Traceability

To scale with confidence, the quantitative logic and the generative layers are versioned independently. A regulatory report references "Copilot v1.2 / Engine v3.1", aiming to ensure full reproducibility across financial years.
