DTCC Full Commercial Launch: Production DLT Clearing

Analysis of the Depository Trust & Clearing Corporation (DTCC) full commercial deployment of its Digital Assets Platform (DAP) across Canton Network, Ethereum, and private banking nodes.

Executive Summary

In October 2026, the DTCC announced the full commercial production launch of its Digital Assets Platform. Transitioning from pilot testing to full clearing operational status, DTCC now processes tokenized U.S. Treasuries, corporate bonds, and repo transactions across inter-connected DLT networks.
