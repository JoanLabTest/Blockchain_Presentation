DTCC Goes Multichain: Tokenized DTC Assets on Solana in 2027

On 12 November 2026, the world's largest post-trade infrastructure announced that its Tokenization Service will connect to Solana. Tokens representing eligible DTC-custodied assets are expected on Solana in the second half of 2027.

Last updated: 18 November 2026 · DCM-RES-2026-017
Executive Summary

DTCC announced on 12 November 2026 that its Tokenization Service will connect with Solana. Tokens representing eligible DTC-custodied assets are expected to be available on Solana in H2 2027. DTCC states that the tokens will keep the same investor protections, ownership rights and regulatory safeguards as traditionally held securities. In parallel, ICE says its trading systems are technically ready to accept tokens issued on Solana, while the planned NYSE digital platform remains subject to regulatory approval.

1. The announcement

What DTCC Actually Committed To

DTCC's 12 November press release states that its Tokenization Service will connect with Solana, so that eligible assets held at DTC can be represented as tokens on that network. According to Brian Steele, DTCC's President of Clearing & Securities Services, the tokens will retain the same investor protections, ownership rights and regulatory safeguards that apply to traditionally held securities.

This builds on the SEC's December 2025 three-year no-action letter authorising DTCC's tokenization programme under specified conditions, and on the service launch of 28 October 2026 on the Canton Network and DTCC's private Besu network (Circle's Arc and Stellar are planned for 2027), covered in our October 2026 analysis and DTS participant review.

2. The exchange layer

ICE/NYSE: Trading Tokens, Not Just Settling Them

On a separate track, ICE says its trading systems are technically ready to accept tokens issued on Solana. NYSE's planned digital platform targets 24/7 trading and immediate settlement but remains subject to regulatory approval. ICE has appointed Securitize as the first qualified digital transfer agent for issuers wishing to issue blockchain-native securities on that platform. NYSE has also filed a rule change with the SEC so that eligible securities can trade as digital tokens under DTCC's pilot.

3. Why it matters

From Single-Ledger Pilots to Multichain Plumbing

  • Public-chain legitimacy: a systemically important US infrastructure connecting to a public network is a strong signal for institutional adoption beyond permissioned ledgers.
  • Collateral mobility: tokenized Treasuries held at DTC could eventually become usable as on-chain collateral, which links to tokenized money-market funds (about $14bn in tokenized US Treasury funds as of late October 2026).
  • Solana momentum in fixed income: the announcement follows Schroders' EM bond fund on Solana and Kazakhstan's notes on Solana (see the Digital Bond Tracker).
  • Standards pressure: multichain issuance strengthens the case for common token standards, which is the focus of Europe's ETSG.
4. Risks

Open Questions for Institutions

  • Operational security: the October 2026 Binance breach (about $6.3bn) is a reminder that key management and wallet operations remain the main point of failure (see Key Infrastructure).
  • Permissioning on a public chain: how transfer restrictions, KYC allow-lists and corporate actions will be enforced.
  • Settlement asset: which cash leg (stablecoins, tokenized deposits or a future wholesale CBDC link) will be accepted.
