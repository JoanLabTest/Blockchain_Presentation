1. Central Infrastructure

DTCC DTS: The 20-Institution Clearing Cartel

On 28 October 2026, the DTCC launched its Digital Tokenization Service (DTS), deploying bidirectional conversion of physical/book-entry securities into tokens across Canton Network and Hyperledger Besu. Twenty institutions connected on day one, including Morgan Stanley, JPMorgan, Citi, Bank of America, Fidelity and Citadel Securities.

This is no longer a bilateral pilot. The DTCC now acts as the central conservatory depository and issuer of the programmable representation of securities. Collateral becomes mobilisable intraday without breaking the legal custody chain, generating an immediate velocity gain across the repo and securities-lending markets.

The Russell 1000 equities, ETFs and US Treasuries processed through DTS represent the first systematic tokenisation of core capital-markets infrastructure. The implications for settlement latency, margin efficiency and cross-border collateral mobility are structural, not incremental.

2. Regulatory Bifurcation

Post-Midterms: Crypto Retail Frozen, Institutional Track Immunised

The Democratic recapture of the House and Senate on 4 November 2026 buries the CLARITY Act before 2029, freezing all broad crypto-retail reform. The bifurcation is now explicit: retail digital-asset legislation is paralysed; institutional securities tokenisation is immunised and advancing through SEC administrative channels (ATS exemptions, transfer-agent modernisation).

The GENIUS Act — passed bipartisanly in 2025 — enters into force on 18 January 2027, locking a de facto monopoly for 100%-liquid-backed, bank-supervised stablecoin issuers. This creates a durable floor for institutional stablecoin adoption, independent of any further legislative action.

For DCM Core, this bifurcation amplifies the value of our comparative regulatory intelligence. As Asia accelerates (Singapore/Project Guardian, Hong Kong/Project Ensemble, South Korea/STO Act, Japan/Progmat), the transatlantic regulatory gap becomes the primary arbitrage vector for institutional capital allocation.

3. RWA Telemetry & Strategic Recommendations

Schroders, RWA Observatory Q4 & Asia-Pacific Execution

Schroders ($1.1 trillion AuM) has tokenised its Luxembourg UCITS Schroder ISF Emerging Market Bonds fund on public Solana blockchain via a feeder-fund structure operated by the Centrifuge protocol. This signals the end of RWA's money-market phase: until now, 70% of institutional tokenisation was confined to cash and Treasuries (BlackRock BUIDL $2.95B, Franklin Templeton BENJI $2.55B).

DCM Core Observatory Q4 telemetry: total RWA capitalisation $39.2B (+13.6% QoQ), with US Treasuries & cash at $14.1B, private credit & high yield at $11.8B (Centrifuge, Maple, Figure, Goldfinch), and real assets & real estate at $4.3B. The Philippines SEC has formalised a tokenised bond and real-estate sandbox; India Blockchain Week 2026 validated combined use of SEBI Demat 2.0 and autonomous AI agents for post-trade settlement.

Three Board recommendations: (1) Structure a paid data-feed offering (API Data / Institutional Feed) for treasury and risk-management desks; (2) Schedule a joint institutional consultation with ESMA and MAS/Philippines SEC on cross-border clearing gateways; (3) Commission a COMEX special report on "Institutional DeFi & DORA" covering validator-node auditability and cryptographic key segregation under DORA/MiCA.
