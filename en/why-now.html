Market Timing

Why Now?

Moving from proof of concept to production at scale in institutional digital assets is no longer a technology question. It is a regulatory imperative driven by 4 concurrent systemic catalysts.

Janvier 2025

DORA Enforcement

The application of the Digital Operational Resilience Act requires EU financial entities to demonstrate their resilience to ICT (information and communication technology) risk.

Impact DCMCore : Automated mapping of DLT dependencies and cryptographic incident reports help meet the requirements on identifying ICT assets (Article 8) and managing ICT-related incidents (Articles 17 to 19).
December 2024

MiCA Full Applicability

The Markets in Crypto-Assets framework now applies in full to crypto-asset service providers (CASPs), with strict rules on governance, conflicts of interest and segregation of assets.

Impact DCMCore : Banks need an "MRM Hub" to show that distributed-ledger risks are actively identified and managed: MiCA requires CASPs to have the arrangements required by DORA and risk assessment procedures (Art. 68(8)).
2024-2025

ECB Wholesale DLT Trials

The European Central Bank is running exploratory trials for settlement in central bank money (CeBM) on DLT, with broad participation from commercial banks.

Impact DCMCore : The move to on-chain DvP settlement creates an urgent need for cross-chain observability (SWIAT, Canton) for intraday liquidity management teams.
1 January 2026 (scheduled)

Basel Committee (BCBS) Prudential Standard

Implementation of the Basel prudential standard on the treatment of crypto-asset exposures (SCO60), with infrastructure risk add-ons and exposure limits for Group 2 crypto-assets (1% of Tier 1, 2% hard limit).

Impact DCMCore : This is the core of our value proposition. Without a precise quantification of a token's infrastructure risk (layer 1, consensus, smart contracts), the bank bears the maximum capital charge.

The investor window of opportunity

The market no longer funds blockchain ideology; it funds compliance infrastructure. Global systemically important banks (G-SIBs) have redirected their "innovation" budgets towards "regulatory readiness".
